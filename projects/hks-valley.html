Valley Children
Hospital Expansion

A Vibrant New Addition to the Campus

Location California, USA
Type Healthcare
Role Concept, Design Options, 3D Model, Rendering Coordination, Diagram
Team HKS LA Healthcare

The new pediatric clinic establishes a welcoming gateway at the intersection of Peck and Guilds Boulevards, strengthening the identity of the Valley Children’s campus.

The building is designed at a child’s scale — creating spaces that feel approachable, comfortable, and engaging rather than institutional. Natural daylight, lower seating elements, integrated play opportunities, and visual connections to the outdoors support curiosity, calmness, and emotional well-being.
