Westwood Village
Vision Plan

A New Chapter for a Storied Los Angeles Neighborhood

Location Los Angeles, USA
Type Urban Design
Start Fall 2022
Role Concept, Design Options, 3D Model, Rendering Coordination, Diagram
Team HKS SF CMU Team

The Westwood Village Vision Plan defines forward-looking development opportunities to restore and transform this storied neighborhood. The goal is to celebrate the heart and soul of Westwood Village while preserving its unique character and architectural heritage.

The plan fosters distinct yet interconnected experiences along Westwood Boulevard, Broxton Avenue, and Glendon Avenue. Transit-oriented housing leverages new public transit investment to create a more inclusive and connected neighborhood.
