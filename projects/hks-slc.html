Team HKS SLC Culture

Block 78 sits between the Delta Center and Convention Center, creating a rare opportunity to transform an inward-facing site into a vibrant urban district. Rooted in the area’s multicultural history, the district reimagines Block 78 as a place for gathering, discovery, and civic life.

A connected network of plazas, terraces, paseos, and pocket gardens weaves nature throughout the district, creating moments of gathering and relaxation at every scale.
